What works with no connection

Still works: working out costs from miles you type into the small boxes, changing the car, season, tariff and petrol price, nicknames and saved places, saving, starring and deleting journeys, and looking at your saved journeys. Everything you do is kept on this device.

Needs internet: automatic road miles between postcodes, sharing with family (your changes wait on this device and sync once you are back online), and sending a WhatsApp or email. The app must have been opened once online before it can open with no signal.

If only the sharing server is down: the internet still works, so road miles and everything else carry on. Only the family sync waits.

After 3 minutes with no connection this bar turns red to remind you that live road miles, sharing and tariff updates are unavailable. The calculator itself keeps working.

Settings: cars, home, petrol, tariffs, sharing

Home

Home is the default starting point and is listed first in every "Saved place" list. It is kept on this device, and is separate from petrol prices.

Cars

Miles per kWh. Figures marked "guess" are my estimates of real-world use. Overtype any of them and they become yours.

The built-in database (Open EV Data, licence CDLA-Permissive-2.0) holds about 700 electric cars from 2023 to 2025 with their official battery size and range. Older cars are often missing: add them as typed and enter your own miles per kWh.

Saved places

Places you saved with a nickname. They appear in the Saved place drop-down on each route point.

Petrol and charging

Charging losses add the energy lost between the wall and the battery (typically 8 to 12%). Leave at 0 to match simple mile-per-kWh sums.

Electricity suppliers

Pence per kWh. A rough guide only; check each supplier's own quote for your postcode.
