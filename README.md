# MyEV Calculator

Works out what a journey costs in an electric car compared with petrol: road miles between postcodes (with up to two stops and an optional straight-back return), real-world miles per kWh by season, and your electricity tariff. Journeys and settings are saved in your own browser only.

Open it here: https://iangough.github.io/myev-calculator/

Car list data: Open EV Data (https://github.com/open-ev-data/open-ev-data-dataset), licence CDLA-Permissive-2.0. Road miles use postcodes.io and OpenStreetMap routing. Efficiency figures are estimates: overtype them with your own.
