Suggested places

These appear in every phone's "Saved place" list under "Suggested places".

Starter routes

Each phone gets a "Starter routes" list. A route is a name, a start, an end, optional stops, and whether it returns.

From, to and stops can be a postcode or one of your suggested place nicknames above. The word Home means each person's own home.

Notice for everyone

A short message shown at the top of every phone until the person dismisses it. Leave it empty to show nothing.

Who has read the published notice

Shows the notice that is live now, not edits you have not published yet. A phone is listed when it has opened the app and shown the notice ("Seen"), and again when the person taps the cross ("Dismissed"). Names appear only where the person typed a name in Settings, Home; others show as an anonymous device code. Phones that have not opened the app since the notice went out cannot be listed, because the service does not know them.

Chintzy (cheapest petrol app)

Chintzy is the companion to MyEV: it lists the 10 cheapest petrol stations near a postcode and can send price-drop alerts. The buttons on this tab take effect straight away; they do not need Publish changes. No names are stored: each phone is only an anonymous alert address with a rounded location.
